\section{Experiments and Results}
\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate SVGD variants on five benchmarks: a 50D Gaussian target, a suite of
2D synthetic targets, UCI logistic regression, a 2D Gaussian mixture (mix8),
and a small Bayesian neural network (BNN). Across all experiments we report
effective sample size (ESS), KSD when available, and task-specific metrics.
Early stopping uses KSD when it degrades persistently to avoid late-run
instabilities.

\subsection{50D Gaussian}

This high-dimensional benchmark stresses stability and scaling. We optionally
whiten coordinates using the target covariance to stabilize drift and repulsion.
We report moment errors ($\|\hat{\mu}-\mu\|_2$ and
$\|\hat{\Sigma}-\Sigma\|_F$), ESS, KSD, and mean log probability.
