\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We presented multirate SVGD variants that split drift and repulsion to improve
stability and efficiency on stiff targets. Across synthetic and real-world
benchmarks, multirate splitting and adaptive drift substepping consistently
improve robustness relative to vanilla SVGD, while remaining competitive with
SGLD and SGHMC. The results highlight the importance of separating time scales
in particle interactions, especially in high dimensions and multimodal
settings.

Future work includes more principled adaptivity for repulsion frequency,
approximate kernel interactions for large $N$, and tighter links to classical
multirate stability theory for particle-based Bayesian inference.
